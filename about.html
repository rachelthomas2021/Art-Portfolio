ABOUT THE STUDIO

Hi, I'm
Rachel Keys

I've been drawing for 10 years, and I still love finding new ways to bring color and personality to the things around us.

I'm especially drawn to ordinary objects and the small details of everyday life. With a little imagination, they can become the start of a lovely pattern.

I love making patterns and creating art that feels simple and soft, with colors and details that feel thoughtful rather than overwhelming. I hope my work brings a little everyday joy to the things it finds its way onto.
